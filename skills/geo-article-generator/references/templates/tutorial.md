# Template — Tutorial / Guide

Read when Information Need is **Learn (how-to)** or **Fix**. Compose from
`../retrieval-units.md`.

## Skeleton

```text
Lead (Summary: outcome + prerequisites)
├── Checklist (requirements)
├── Timeline / numbered steps
├── FAQ (failure modes / Troubleshooting)
└── Sources
```

## Rules

- One step chunk answers "what do I do at this step?" — not a dump of theory.
- Troubleshooting: one FAQ item or step per error class.
- Prefer Checklist + Timeline units over long narrative.
