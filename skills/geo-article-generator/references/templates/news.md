# Template — News

Read when Information Need is **Learn** about a **time-bound** event.
Compose from `../retrieval-units.md`. Keep short.

## Skeleton

```text
Lead (Summary: what happened + when + who)
├── Definition / context (only if needed for the entity)
├── Evidence (announcement source)
├── FAQ (optional: impact questions)
└── Sources
```

## Rules

- Lead must include date/timeframe when materials provide it.
- Do not expand into a full Explainer unless intent shifts.
- No speculative impact claims without sources.
